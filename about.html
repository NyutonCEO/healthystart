Compliance

Healthy Start uses compliance-minded workflows for transportation coordination, payment handling, and partner communication.

HIPAA-conscious processes

We limit protected health information on web forms and use secure coordination channels for sensitive transportation details.

PCI DSS payment handling

Payment workflows are designed to protect cardholder data and avoid storing payment details in website forms.

Clear documentation

Ride details, service expectations, and partner workflows are documented to support accountability and continuity of care.

Policies

Our service policies are written to keep scheduling, rider expectations, cancellations, accessibility needs, and communication standards clear.

Confidentiality

Client information is protected and shared only with authorized staff who need it to coordinate, manage, or deliver services.

Service standards

Transportation services are delivered according to agreed service expectations, applicable standards, and documented partner workflows.

Records and responsibility

Program records, ride documentation, and authorization details are maintained to support accountability and service quality.

For the full policy details, visit Company Policies.

Pricing

Healthy Start provides straightforward fare guidance so riders and partners can understand expected costs before scheduling.

$20 per ride

In-town rides start at $20 per ride, subject to trip details and confirmed service requirements.

$4.50 per mile

Out-of-town transportation is quoted at $4.50 per mile, with final pricing confirmed before service.
