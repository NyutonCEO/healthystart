Provider Standards

Healthy Start maintains service policies that support reliable transportation, accurate documentation, confidentiality, accessibility, and compliance with applicable program requirements.

Service delivery

Provide transportation services as agreed and in accordance with applicable service standards.

Program documentation

Maintain financial, operational, and program records needed to support service delivery, reimbursement, and quality review.

Client confidentiality

Protect client information and share it only with authorized staff who need it to coordinate, manage, or deliver transportation services.

Licensing and standards

Follow applicable state licensing standards, accrediting standards, and service criteria established for quality transportation support.

Accessibility and nondiscrimination

Support accessibility requirements, including Section 504 principles, and prohibit discrimination in service operations.

Civil rights compliance

Operate in alignment with applicable civil rights requirements, including Title VI and Title VII principles.

Records and Responsibility

Clear records and accountable service practices help partners, riders, and care teams understand what was scheduled, authorized, and delivered.

Service records

Maintain appropriate ride, program, and client service records that document authorized services and completed transportation support.

Fiscal responsibility

Accept responsibility for service deviations caused by provider actions, officers, employees, agents, or representatives.

Ride Expectations

These operating policies help riders and partners plan transportation with fewer delays and clearer expectations.

Scheduling

Book at least 24 hours in advance when possible so vehicle type, pickup timing, and mobility needs can be confirmed.

Cancellations and changes

Call as soon as plans change so we can adjust routing, release vehicle capacity, and communicate with affected parties.

Payment handling

Payment details are confirmed through approved channels. Do not include card numbers or sensitive payment data in website forms.
